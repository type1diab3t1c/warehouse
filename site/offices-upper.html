Operations & Management

Upper Floor Offices

The upper floor houses three offices designed for backend operations and management. Accessed via an interior staircase from the ground-floor reception, this level is separated from customer-facing activity and purpose-built for operational oversight. Two of the three offices feature windows overlooking the warehouse loading bays, allowing supervisors and managers to maintain direct visual contact with floor operations. The suite includes a full kitchen for extended work days and a private bathroom with modern finishes.

Level: Second floor, accessed via interior staircase
Layout: Three private offices, two with warehouse bay overlook
Amenities: Full kitchen, private bathroom
Best For: Operations management, logistics coordination, back-office processing
